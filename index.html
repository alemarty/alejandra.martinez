Alejandra Martinez

Hello, and welcome to my website!

I am an economist affiliated with the Centre for Inclusive Trade Policy (CITP). My research focuses on empirical international trade, with an emphasis on firm-to-firm dynamics, production networks and firm heterogeneity. I am also interested in market competition and trade policy interventions.

I have worked at the University of Nottingham (affiliated with GEP), the World Bank, CREST and CEDE. I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Warwick in 2024, and my MA (2014) and BA (2012) in Economics from Universidad de los Andes.

Beyond research, I follow global events and occasionally write shorter, non-academic pieces on topics I find interesting.
